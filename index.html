How to play

Goal

Tap, click or press Space to drop the sliding block onto the tower. Anything that hangs over the edge is cut off, so the tower gets narrower the less accurate you are. Miss the tower completely and it is game over.

Perfect drops

Land a block almost exactly on top of the one below and it snaps into place with nothing cut off. Three perfect drops in a row start growing your block back, up to its starting width.

Speed

The blocks slide faster as the tower gets taller.

Daily

The daily challenge uses the same speeds for everyone and changes at 00:00 UTC. Reach 30 layers to clear it and keep your daily streak. You can retry as often as you like.

Supporters

A tip of 0.01 XNO or more unlocks the Gold, Neon and Mint block themes. Tips are optional and the whole game is free.
